Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Your calculations stay on your device. PriceMate works without an account and does not upload your calculation inputs, results, history, favorites, or preferences to me or to an external calculation service.

1. About this policy

My name is Darell Fergus. I am the sole independent developer of PriceMate. This app is developed and maintained by me personally, not by a company or team. References to “I,” “me,” and “my” in this policy mean Darell Fergus.

This Privacy Policy explains how PriceMate handles information when you use the Android or iOS application. PriceMate is an everyday calculation toolkit for discounts, tax, profit, markup, bill splitting, percentages, and price comparisons. This policy describes the current app functionality.

2. Information stored on your device

PriceMate processes the values you enter on your device to calculate and display results. To remember your activity and preferences, it stores the following information in local app storage:

  • Calculation history: up to your 500 most recent completed calculations, including calculator type, entered values, selected calculation options, results, and date and time.
  • Favorites: the calculators you mark as favorites.
  • Display preferences: your selected currency symbol, light or dark appearance, and decimal display preference.

This information is stored locally for the app to function. I do not receive or maintain a server-side copy of it. PriceMate does not require your name, email address, phone number, bank details, or account registration.

3. How information is used

PriceMate uses locally stored information to:

  • Perform calculations using the values you enter.
  • Show results and calculation breakdowns.
  • Save and reopen previous calculations.
  • Provide quick access to favorite and recently used calculators.
  • Remember your currency and display preferences.

Currency selection changes only the displayed symbol. It does not convert amounts or connect to a live exchange-rate service. All seven calculators work without an internet connection.

4. Data sharing, advertising, and tracking

PriceMate does not upload or share your calculation data with me, advertisers, or external calculation services. I do not sell or rent your calculation data.

The app does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, cross-app tracking, payment processing, or a cloud database for your calculations.

Your operating system, app store, and device backup services operate separately from PriceMate and may process information under their own privacy policies and your platform settings.

5. Data retention and your controls

Locally stored information remains available until you remove it, older calculations are replaced by the history limit, or local app storage is removed.

You can manage your information directly in the app:

  • Delete individual calculations from History.
  • Clear all calculation history from History or Settings.
  • Remove favorites by tapping their stars.
  • Change your currency and display preferences in Settings.
  • Reset preferences to their default values.

Resetting preferences does not delete history or favorites. Clearing history does not reset preferences or remove favorites.

Removing the app generally removes its local storage. On platforms that support it, clearing the app’s storage also removes its locally stored data. Backup copies may remain until you manage or delete them through your backup provider.

Because I do not have access to your locally stored calculations, I cannot retrieve or delete those records remotely. Use the controls in the app or your device settings to manage them.

6. Device backups and security

Your operating system may include PriceMate’s local app data in device or cloud backups, depending on your settings. Restoring a backup may restore previously saved app data. I do not operate or control these backup services.

You can review and manage backups through your device or backup provider’s settings.

Local storage does not guarantee absolute security. Protect your device with a passcode or other appropriate security measures and avoid entering information you do not want stored on that device. PriceMate is not intended as a secure vault for financial credentials or other sensitive records.

7. Children’s privacy

PriceMate does not require personal information or an account from any user, including children. I do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app’s calculator functionality.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to me through a separate communication, please contact me using the details below.

8. Information you send when contacting me

If you contact me by email, I may receive your email address, name if supplied, and any information you choose to include. I use that information to respond to your request and address the issue you report.

Support communications are separate from the app’s local calculation storage. Please avoid sending sensitive financial information or calculation records unless necessary for your request.

Email providers may process communications under their own privacy policies. I retain support communications only as reasonably necessary to handle your request and meet any applicable legal obligations. You may contact me to request deletion of information you have sent, subject to those obligations.

9. This policy’s hosting

If you view this policy on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical information about your visit, such as your IP address and request details, under its own privacy policy. Visiting this page does not give GitHub access to your locally stored PriceMate calculations.

This policy page does not include analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by this page, or external fonts. For GitHub’s practices, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement .

10. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s functionality or information-handling practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
